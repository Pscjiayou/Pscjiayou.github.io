## Rockets and space

I love rockets and space exploration. During a trip to Orlando, I visited NASA and had the chance to see these enormous rocket engines up close. It's one thing to read about them, and quite another to stand beneath them!

Since childhood, I've been inspired by the curiosity, courage, and determination behind humanity's journey into space. To me, it's an epic story of exploration, one that reminds me to dream big, take small steps, and keep moving forward.
